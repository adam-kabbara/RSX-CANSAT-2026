EEPROM pin connections -> Arduino Uno:

    1: CS (Chip Select) -> D10          | CS/SS
    2: SO (Master In Slave Out) -> D12  | MISO
    3: WP -> GND
    4: 0V (Ground)-> GND
    5: SI (Master Out Slave In) -> D11  | MOSI
    6: SCK -> D13                       | SCK
    7: HOLD -> 3.3V
    8: Vcc -> 3.3V

*5V also works
